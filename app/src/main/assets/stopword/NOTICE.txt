The stop-word model in this directory (stop.tflite plus its stop.json manifest) is an unmodified copy of
the assets of release "stop" ("Stop" model beta 20241017.5) of https://github.com/kahrendt/microWakeWord
(stop.tflite SHA-256 b5a18c4ad681a89950dfade31011e1631bdcb333e93c84519a1a63ff4f071146, stop.json SHA-256
bd13aeb1b83852649dc4fb6135cb160ff68716d14612b06f6a405342c57447aa), licensed under the Apache License 2.0
reproduced in the adjacent LICENSE.txt. Its manifest names the model's author.

It runs on the same native engine as the wake words, documented in app/src/main/cpp/microwakeword/THIRD_PARTY.md.
tools/wakeword/vendor-upstream.sh does not fetch it; re-vendor it by hand from that release and check both digests.
